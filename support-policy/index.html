Support Policy

Paperlatch · Last updated 19 October 2026

This policy applies to every Paperlatch app on the Atlassian Marketplace, on every plan, including free tiers and evaluations.

How to reach us

Email support@paperlatch.com. Email is our only support channel, so every request has a written record you can attach to your own files. Each app's support page lists what to include for a useful first reply.

Hours of operation

DaysMonday to Friday
Hours09:00–17:00, Israel time (Asia/Jerusalem)
HolidaysIsraeli public holidays. Requests received on a holiday are answered on the next business day.

Response times

First responseWithin 48 hours (two business days)
Security reportsAcknowledged within two business days, initial assessment within five. See the Security Policy.
Bugs that show an incorrect compliance resultTreated as our highest priority. We will tell you when a fix ships.

A first response is a reply from us that addresses your request, not an automatic acknowledgement. Some requests need more than one exchange; we will tell you what we are doing and when you can expect to hear from us next.

What support covers

  • Installing, configuring and using our apps
  • Bugs, and questions about what a report or export means
  • Data, privacy and security questions, including deletion requests

Our apps run entirely inside your own Atlassian site, so we cannot see your data. We may ask for screenshots or an export to understand a problem.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will change.
