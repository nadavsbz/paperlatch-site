Small, focused apps for Confluence

Each one does a single job and stays out of the way of the people using it.

Our apps run on Forge, inside Atlassian, so your data stays in your own Atlassian site. We ask for the narrowest set of permissions an app needs to do its job, and you can check that on the Permissions tab of any of our listings rather than take our word for it.

Confirm & Chase

Read confirmation for policies and SOPs. Readers confirm a page once; you get one dashboard covering every policy across every space, showing what is clear, what is outstanding, and who still has to confirm. Change the policy and everyone is asked again, while earlier rounds stay on record for your auditor.
