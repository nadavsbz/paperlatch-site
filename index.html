Built to be easy for a security team to approve

Small apps. Small permissions.

  • Runs on Atlassian Forge, inside Atlassian
  • No application server of ours, and no external storage of your page content
  • Read-only access to your content; the only thing it writes is its own confirmation records
  • No acting on a user's behalf
  • No access to email addresses, and no advertising or behavioural analytics in the app

Every one of those is checkable on the Permissions tab of a Marketplace listing. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored.
