Our apps

Confirm & Chase

Read confirmation and policy acknowledgement for Confluence. Readers confirm a page once; you get one dashboard covering every policy across every space, showing what is clear, what is outstanding, and who still needs chasing. Change the policy and everyone is asked again, while earlier rounds stay on record for your auditor.

Page Approval & Document Sign-off

Document approval for Confluence. Approvers approve or reject the exact page version they read; when the page is edited, it shows that it is no longer approved, and the earlier decisions stay on record. One list of what is waiting, with a CSV export. Approvers need only read access.

Built to be easy for a security team to approve

Small apps. Small permissions.

  • Runs on Atlassian Forge, inside Atlassian
  • No application server of ours, and no external storage of your page content
  • Neither app writes to your content. Each stores only its own records — confirmations in one, approval decisions in the other
  • No acting on a user's behalf
  • No advertising and no behavioural analytics in either app

Every one of those is checkable on the Permissions tab of a Marketplace listing, which is the point of stating them this way rather than asking you to take our word for it.

The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored.
