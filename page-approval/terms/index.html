3. What the app provides, and what remains yours

The app records approval decisions on Confluence pages, binds each one to a page version by a content hash, shows when a page has changed since it was approved, checks the integrity of the record, and exports it.

The app does not lock pages and does not make your process compliant with any standard or regulation. It is not a regulated electronic signature. Among the things that remain yours:

  • Controls over Confluence itself: who can edit pages, who administers the site, and how accounts are issued and removed. Anyone who can edit a page can change its approvers; every change is recorded.
  • Telling approvers that a page is waiting. The app shows it on its own page; it does not send email or notifications.
  • Deciding whether an approval in the app is sufficient for your procedures.

The approved content is the page title and body (Confluence storage format) at the decided version. Attachments, comments and labels are not part of it, and the app says so where decisions are shown.
