3. What the app does not promise

The app records the events Confluence sends it, from the moment it is installed. It does not reconstruct history from before installation, and it cannot: those events were never emitted to it and no API returns them afterwards.

This matters if you intend to rely on these records as evidence. Three limits are inherent to the platform rather than to our implementation, and we state them here as well as in the app itself:

  • The record begins at installation.
  • Confluence does not emit cascading deletion events, so a page removed together with its space produces no page-level event. The app reports it from the space record and labels it as an inference, not as a recorded event.
  • Confluence does not collect a reason when content is deleted, so no app can report one.

Where the app cannot record something, it stores a gap marker and the report shows it. We consider that a feature, and it is also a warning: a trail showing gaps is telling you it is incomplete, and you should treat it that way.

Whether this record satisfies a particular auditor, regulator, standard or court is a question about your compliance programme, not about this app, and we make no representation about it.
